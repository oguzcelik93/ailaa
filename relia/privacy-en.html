Privacy policy

Last updated: 18.08.2026

In short

Relia does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, phone number or location. Your checks stay on your device. Only what you deliberately send reaches the server.

Stored on your device

  • Completed checks and cars saved to your garage
  • Answers of an unfinished check
  • A randomly generated device ID, not linked to your identity
  • Your language and unit preference

This data stays in your device storage and is not sent to us. You can erase all of it from Settings with "Delete my data".

Sent to the server

  • If you submit a scam report: listing number, listing site, make and model, your description, your evidence photos and your device ID
  • If you choose to share your check result: listing number, make and model, score and number of findings. No photos or notes
  • If you create a report link or card image: the page or image you generated. Anyone with the link can view it; deleted after 90 days
  • If you sign in to the moderation panel: your email and password (stored encrypted)

Reports are deleted after 365 days, report and card files after 90 days.

What we never collect

We do not collect names, phone numbers, addresses, location, contacts, advertising IDs or usage analytics. There are no ads and no third-party trackers in the app.

Camera and photos

Camera access is used only when you choose to add a photo. Photos taken during a check stay on your device; they are uploaded only if you attach them as evidence to a report.

Infrastructure

Data is stored on Supabase servers within the European Union. Fonts and the interface library load from a content delivery network; your IP address is visible to those providers during those requests.

Your rights

You can erase your device data at any time from Settings. To have a report removed from the server, contact us; we will handle the request within 30 days.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
